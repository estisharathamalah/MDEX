# IP / Defensibility Brief — MDEX

## Existing scientific foundations (not proprietary)
Discrete Bayesian updating, Shannon entropy as an uncertainty measure, Value-of-Information decision analysis (Howard-style pre-posterior VOI), and expected monetary value calculations are all long-established, publicly known techniques. MDEX makes no claim to have invented any of these.

## MDEX-specific technical integration (candidate defensibility basis)
- The specific coupling of a geological hypothesis belief state, a VOI engine, an exploration-economics engine, and a hard temporal-firewall data model into one auditable, replayable decision loop.
- The temporal-firewall data model itself (provenance + `date_observed`/`date_available` on every evidence item, structurally enforced via a single sanctioned read path) as a discipline for historically honest algorithm evaluation in a domain (mineral exploration) where this is not standard practice.
- The evaluation methodology that explicitly reports disagreement between the model and historical human decisions rather than tuning the model to agree with history.

## Proprietary methodology / architecture (candidate, unproven)
`[UNPROVEN]` The specific combination rule, likelihood-elicitation process, and multi-case validation protocol MDEX would develop through further work could constitute proprietary know-how. At the current TRL 3 stage this is a hypothesis, not an established asset.

## Potential trade secrets
If MDEX is developed further, likely trade-secret candidates are: elicited/fitted likelihood tables built from proprietary or licensed historical exploration data, and any resulting multi-case validation corpus and its calibration results — not the underlying mathematics.

## Proprietary datasets
None currently exist. This POC deliberately avoids treating the illustrative synthetic scenario data as a proprietary dataset; a real proprietary dataset would need to be built from licensed or partner-provided exploration data under appropriate agreements.

## Possible patentable subject matter
`[UNPROVEN]`. Method claims around the specific integration of temporal-firewalled evidence provenance with a VOI-driven sequential exploration decision architecture are the most plausible candidate, but no patentability search or legal analysis has been performed. **No patentability claim is made here.**

## IP risks
- Novelty risk: decision-support systems combining Bayesian updating and VOI exist in adjacent domains (petroleum, medical decision-making); a patentability study must assess prior art specifically in mineral exploration decision support.
- Freedom-to-operate has not been assessed.

## Recommended future patentability study
Engage a patent attorney with mining-tech or decision-science experience to conduct a prior-art search focused on (a) VOI-based exploration decision support systems, and (b) temporally-firewalled historical backtesting methodologies in geoscience, before any filing decision.
