# Market Brief — MDEX

**No customer interviews, letters of intent, revenue figures, or market-size numbers have been produced for this brief. None currently exist. Everything below is framing and hypothesis, explicitly marked, pending real validation (see "Customer validation requirements").**

## Mineral exploration market problem
Exploration capital allocation decisions (where to drill next, whether to survey or stop) are made under uncertainty with limited quantified decision support, and are difficult to audit after the fact — a structural problem independent of company size, most acute for capital-constrained junior explorers where a single wrong deep hole can be a meaningful fraction of a raise.

## Target customers `[UNPROVEN — hypothesis]`
- Junior/mid-tier exploration companies making discrete go/no-go drilling decisions under tight budgets.
- Major mining companies' exploration technical teams seeking an auditable decision-support layer for board/investor reporting.
- Government or sovereign geological survey bodies evaluating exploration licence portfolios.

## Mining use cases `[UNPROVEN — hypothesis]`
Next-action recommendation under budget constraint; retrospective audit of past campaign decisions; portfolio-level triage across multiple licence areas competing for the same budget.

## Value proposition `[UNPROVEN — hypothesis]`
A quantified, auditable, and replayable rationale for exploration spend, potentially improving capital efficiency and giving boards/investors a clearer decision trail than tacit geologist judgment alone. This value proposition has not yet been tested with real customers.

## Saudi opportunity `[UNPROVEN — hypothesis, requires local validation]`
Saudi Arabia's mineral sector expansion (Vision 2030 mining diversification, Ma'aden's growth, new exploration licensing activity) plausibly creates a domestic customer base for exploration decision-support tooling, and a KFUPM/Dhahran Techno Valley base plausibly aids access to Saudi Geological Survey and Ma'aden-adjacent stakeholders. This has not been validated with any actual conversation in this session.

## International expansion / Africa hypothesis `[UNPROVEN — hypothesis]`
Copper-gold and battery-metal exploration activity in Africa (DRC, Zambia, West African gold belt) is often cited as an analogous capital-constrained, decision-support-underserved market; this is asserted as a hypothesis for future validation, not a researched go-to-market plan.

## Commercial model `[UNPROVEN — hypothesis]`
Plausible options to evaluate, not commitments: per-campaign consulting/licensing engagement; SaaS-style seat licensing for exploration technical teams; data/insight-sharing partnership models with exploration companies willing to contribute historical data for likelihood calibration.

## Customer validation requirements (what must happen before any of the above is treated as validated)
1. Structured discovery interviews with exploration technical managers at 2–3 junior and 1–2 major companies.
2. At least one real pilot using an active or recently closed exploration program's own data, run prospectively or near-prospectively.
3. Direct engagement with Saudi mineral-sector stakeholders (e.g., Saudi Geological Survey, Ma'aden exploration division) to test the Saudi-market hypothesis specifically.
